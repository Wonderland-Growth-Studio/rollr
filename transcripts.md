## Recurring themes across interviews

Short index for navigating the transcripts below. Verify against the quoted text before using in copy.

- **First impression is weight and substance.** Katie ("this has got substance"), Tom ("the heft... a proper thing"), Lucy ("don't compromise on the weight"), Adrian ("looks nice and robust"), Kieran (bottle "mysterious, compact, sturdy").
- **Aesthetics drive discovery and purchase; sustainability is a bonus, not the driver.** Joy (Facebook ad, dressing-table shot; "wrong age to be bothered about sustainability"), Katie ("definitely not the driver"), Liz ("I like nice things"), Adrian ("a treat for himself"). Tom, Dawn, Kieran and Sangeeta are the exceptions where refillability and the tiny paper sachets matter in their own right. Sangeeta also wanted a British, low-carbon brand and found the website "quietly influential" versus AKT, which "shouted".
- **Rollr is framed as skincare or perfume, not antiperspirant.** Katie ("feels more like skin care than anti-sweat", "cared for"), Dawn ("like perfume for your armpits"), Joy (compares scents to perfume and wants more feminine, "girly" options), Sangeeta (rolls it on as perfume; a daily ritual of gratitude; "nature knows I'm doing the right thing"), Kieran (chooses scent like perfume, so not smelling first is the blocker).
- **Efficacy surprises people who expected natural deodorant to fail.** Katie (ex-Mitchum), Joy (post-menopause, "I do not smell"), Tom (longest lasting of the naturals; Fussy gave him a rash). Lucy is the counterpoint: sweatier family members found it did not work until she changed the liquid monthly. Sangeeta: lasts all day, including hot climates and nervous meetings.
- **Lid and grip.** Liz (shiny, no purchase, risk of dropping on basin), Katie (black lid feels masculine; wants matching coloured or premium lids), Tom (fear of smashing a tile or sink; suggests rubber base). Liz advises against adding lid colour SKUs. Kieran and Sangeeta both say the plastic cradle or cap looks cheap next to the glass and suggest metal, ceramic or a gold detail; Kieran also reports the cradle loosening over time and a replacement lid.
- **Travel and leaking.** Katie (liquid escapes on planes), Lucy (too heavy and delicate to travel with; suggests a pouch), Kieran (leaked a third into the cap on first trip; now leaves it at home; wants a smaller travel size at £15 to £20), Sangeeta (nervous about the glass but now wraps it and takes it; suggests saying "travel friendly" on site).
- **Refill mixing and the water question.** Tom (powder sticks in corners; add a little water first), Joy (tap water "gave me the ick", used filtered water; suggests reassurance copy on site), Kieran (same corner-residue problem and same fix; first-fill instructions unclear).
- **Scent sampling and discovery.** Katie (scratch and sniff), Liz (let people smell before buying; pronunciation guide for gemstone names; call dumortierite "blue quartz"), Tom (samples as a growth lever), Kieran (starter pack of small refills at lower price; sample sachets on big orders; scent chatbot was "generic" and should paint a picture).
- **Price.** Adrian (refill at £8 ideal, £12 "quite a lot"; cost vs Wild), Dawn (anchors on price per refill, not longevity), Joy ("if it was double the money, I would pay it"), Liz (refills "insignificant" after investing in the bottle), Kieran (did the refill maths before buying; bottle price stopped a second purchase and makes it a poor gift because scent is a guess).
- **Subscription edge cases.** Liz (two homes, two bottles, one subscription needed), Dawn and Tom (reorder trigger is opening the last sachet).
- **Word of mouth.** Katie and Lucy both gifted Rollr widely; Katie: "never go back brand". Sangeeta refuses the referral scheme because it feels like selling; she advocates to "pay it forward" and plans to gift one. Her husband and son found the bottle "too girly", the opposite of most feedback.
- **Partnership ideas from customers.** Sangeeta (breast cancer charity tie-in for October with Rose Quartz; spas; beauty awards), Lucy (niece placed Rollr into Six Senses goodie bags).
