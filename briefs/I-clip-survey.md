### The recommendation that follows

A library cut from Cristina + Emily + Laura Kosinskaite + Beth/Andreea **will** show the mechanic,
the price frame and the close working as a coherent film in one caption system — genuinely useful.
**It will not test B3**, because the two beats it must leave out are the two both panels rated.

bathroom, a Rollr and a genuinely unbranded white stick. Cheapest possible shoot, and it buys the
entire film. **It also solves the unbranded-stick problem permanently, since every conventional
stick currently in the library is a named brand.**

## C3 — ~10%, and the Steel constraint is what kills it

**Hannah Rooney's is the only verified Steel ball in the library** (mirror-polished metal at 4K,
28.8–30.5). Everything else is rose quartz or green aventurine. Thirteen further files are tagged
"unknown — could be Steel or a dark stone" and none resolves.

**Beth's master is cut off before the line it was wanted for.** The agent's survey proposed Beth
19.4–26.8 for *"I leave it on the side of my bathroom, and it's earned its place there"*. Her master
runs 26.8s and the transcript ends mid-sentence at *"by the time I leave the bathroom I've had
tha…"*, so the closing lines are not reliably inside it. Use Emily 33.9–39.4 instead — *"Every
morning I reach for it and remember that I choose this for myself"* — which is a verified separate
transcript segment over the bottle standing alone on a ledge.

**And Hannah's clip is unusable as a Steel build anyway:** she has a **Mitchum** in frame and on skin
at 6.0–10.5, and her own VO at 15.3–17.3 says *"Rollr is carved from real gemstone or steel"* — the
banned word, in the mouth of the only Steel creator.

**One beat lands.** Beat 3's shelf of mismatched objects: `Jess Bell_TooManyUglyThings_H2_TheCirqle.mov`
· `1I6u83vyQa9XxtPFWVw8rDGBiJ22oR5en` · **14.0–19.0** — she opens a mirrored cabinet crammed with
mismatched toiletries. Audio 14.0–17.0: *"…and then you go into your bathroom and you open up your
bathroom cabinet, and it looks like this."* No Rollr in frame, so **Steel-safe**. ⚠ Every bottle in
the cabinet is somebody else's brand.

**Beat 1's light exists and is barred.** Cher's clip is genuinely low-light, lamp-lit — luma 51, min
16 — *exactly* C3's "nothing is lit well, on purpose". But her ball is **rose quartz** and her VO
says *"a rose quartz roller"*. Barred from a Steel cut on both counts.

**Beat 4 is the one thing C3 does that the whole library does the opposite of.** *"I thought what I
wanted was a nicer-looking one. That wasn't it."* No creator in the library raises and rejects the
aesthetic answer — every one of them lands **on** it.

**Nothing in the library bins anything**, so the turn is categorically net-new.

> **A library C3 would be a Steel film with a rose-quartz ball in it, which is worse than no film.**
> **If C3 is to be tested cheaply, respec it to the gemstone £45 build** — then Cher's dim bathroom
> and all four refill sequences come back in, and it becomes roughly as buildable as C1.
