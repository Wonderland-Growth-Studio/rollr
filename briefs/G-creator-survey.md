# Creator survey — the material I should have been cutting from

**Written because the question "why are you not considering other creators at all?" had no good
answer.** I anchored on the first four creators I happened to fetch — Caz, Laura May, Chloe H,
Cher Lourens — and never widened. Everything in `E-dr-the-arc.md` V1–V6 was cut from those four.

The cost was not only missing better lines. Two of the four (Caz, Laura May) are **edited files
with captions already burned in**, and from that badly-chosen sample I concluded that a coherent
multi-creator cut was impossible from this library. **That conclusion was wrong**, and it is
corrected below.

## 24 clean creator masters, 14 creators, none restricted

The library holds `*_Twirl.mov` and `*_TheCirqle.mov` files which are **creator masters with no
burned-in captions at all** — as distinct from the edited deliverables (`UGC - Caz`,
`Laura_May_Hook 1`) that carry the agency's subtitles baked into the picture.

## The lines worth building on

### Price — the frame both V5 and V6 leave open

`constraints.md` §1 makes closing the price frame a **hard requirement**, and neither finished film
does it. Two clips close it in the creator's own words:

> **Hannah Rooney** — *"I'd put a £140 perfume on without a second thought, and then put on a
> deodorant that costs less than my morning coffee. That's the first thing on my skin every
> morning, and it didn't even last until lunchtime."*

4K, caption-free, and it is a price-confrontation hook that answers the £3-Sure objection head-on.
This is `C-say-the-price.md` already shot.

> **Laura May, September** — *"It is £45, which is a lot more than I normally spend on a deodorant,
> but if I'm going to use something every single day, I would prefer to love it."*

States the locked £45 and resolves it. (Edited file, burned captions — use the line, find the
master.)

### Openers stronger than what V6 currently uses

## Flags

- **`DO NOT UPLOAD` / `DO NOT LAUNCH` folders** hold Danielle, Hannah and Katie variants. The
  `_Twirl.mov` masters sit outside those folders, but the folder names are an approval signal and
  need a human answer before anything ships.
- **Two `rights: restricted` files**: one Danielle 9:16 and one Hannah 9:16 variant. The `_Twirl`
  masters of both are `unknown`, not restricted — do not confuse them.
- **Cirqle-sourced creators** (Andreea, Patricia, Emily, Shahara, Jess Bell, Amy) are agency
  deliverables and are a separate rights conversation from the direct creators.
- **Hannah's clip shows a competitor-shaped aerosol being binned.** `constraints.md` §4 requires an
  unbranded generic. Check the frames before using that beat.
- **Katie's strongest lines are wetness claims** (*"afterwards I was like, I'm not sweaty"*) and
  **Danielle's insert card reads "Permission to get sweaty."** Both sit in the regulated
  antiperspirant edge banned in §4 without sign-off. Avoid those frames.
- **`Claire_VSL`** carries *"deep perspirant"*, *"reduce sweat"* and a fourth scent (Juniper).
  Three separate breaches of the locked constraints. Do not use.
- **Customer calls** (Vicky, Elizabeth) are genuine named customers — the only non-creator
  testimony in the library, and the only material that is not a paid read. Worth a separate look,
  with the same wetness caution.

## What this changes

1. **A single caption system is available.** Build from the clean masters and caption the whole
   film once, in Rollr type.
2. **The price frame can be closed from existing footage**, which is the hard requirement neither
   V5 nor V6 meets.
3. **Resolution goes up, not down** — eight 4K sources against the 720p that carries most of V5.
4. **V6 should be rebuilt, not patched.** Its foundation is two edited files with incompatible
   burned captions, and its opener cuts a sentence in half.
